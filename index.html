🏛️ Physical Activity & Local Micro-Anthropology

Dokąd? is designed to transform daily walks into meaningful physical wellness and micro-anthropological field exploration.

🎯 Core Principles:

  • Micro-Exploration: Generate 3 non-commercial, serendipitous coordinates in a 1.5 km walking square to uncover architectural heritage, public artifacts, hidden courtyards, and local neighborhood textures.
  • Active Physical Wellness: Encourages steady daily cardio (1.5–3.0 km closed loops) with habit-building streaks and zero gamified screen distraction.
  • Leave No Trace & Ethical Exploring: Only explore public thoroughfares. Respect local communities, private properties, and natural environments.
💼 Ready for portfolio and LinkedIn presentation as an open-source health, civic technology, and urban anthropology project.

🔒 Privacy Policy & Multi-Jurisdictional Notice

Applicable Laws: Poland (RODO / Prawo telekomunikacyjne), Netherlands (AVG / UAVG / Telecommunicatiewet), and United States / Florida (Florida Digital Bill of Rights - Fla. Stat. § 501.701 et seq., CCPA, COPPA).

🛡️ 1. Zero-Telemetry & 100% On-Device Architecture

  • No Central Database or Servers: Dokąd? does not operate user accounts, remote databases, tracking servers, or analytics SDKs. All data remains exclusively on your physical device.
  • Precise Geolocation (GDPR Art. 4 / FDBR § 501.702): GPS coordinates are queried ephemerally in RAM strictly for calculating the 21 m spot arrival proximity. Coordinates are never transmitted, logged, or sold.
  • Biometric Security (GDPR Art. 9 / FDBR § 501.702): WebAuthn Passkeys run inside your device's hardware Secure Enclave / TPM (Windows Hello, Touch/Face ID, Google Password Manager). Biometric data is never accessible to the app or transmitted.
  • Local Photo Vault (OPFS & IndexedDB): Verification photos and memory metadata are written to your local Origin Private File System and IndexedDB. You maintain complete sovereignty over your files.

⚖️ 2. Regional Legal Frameworks

  • 🇵🇱 Poland (Rzeczpospolita Polska): Meets GDPR Art. 5(1)(c) data minimization. Local storage is strictly functional under Art. 173 Prawa telekomunikacyjnego. Freedom of panorama applies under Art. 33 pkt 1 Prawa autorskiego for public architectural scenes.
  • 🇳🇱 Netherlands (Nederland): Meets GDPR & Uitvoeringswet AVG (UAVG). Functional offline caching is exempt under Art. 11.7a Telecommunicatiewet. Freedom of panorama applies under Art. 18 Auteurswet.
  • 🇺🇸 Florida (United States): Complies with the Florida Digital Bill of Rights (Fla. Stat. § 501.701). We provide explicit statutory notice: We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your sensitive personal, biometric, or precise geolocation data. Complies with COPPA (zero collection from children under 13).

📦 3. Data Portability & Complete Erasure

  • Export Anytime: Use the 📥 EXPORT DAY or 📦 EXPORT ALL buttons in the Memories modal to download a full JSON archive of your data.
  • Instant Erasure: Clearing browser site data / storage instantly and permanently deletes 100% of stored photos, streaks, and access keys.
